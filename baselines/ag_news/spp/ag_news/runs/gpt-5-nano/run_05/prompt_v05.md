<task>
Based on the main theme of the given news article, categorize it into exactly one of four topics: World, Sports, Business, or Tech.
</task>
<rules>
- World — international affairs, national politics, government, war, conflict, diplomacy, elections, protests, crime, disasters, accidents, and general or human-interest news. Trade or economic matters belong here when the frame is governmental or geopolitical (sanctions, treaties, inter-state disputes) rather than a specific company or market.
- Sports — any sport, athlete, team, match, league, tournament, or competition, including national-team events.
- Business — companies, markets, share prices, earnings, deals, mergers, the economy, trade, and jobs, when the article's primary subject is the commercial or financial event itself (most often for a non-technology company).
- Tech — science AND technology: scientific research and discovery, nature and the environment, space programs and agencies (e.g. NASA), medical and biological research, gadgets, computing, software, telecom, and the internet. A science, nature, or space story belongs in Tech even when it reads like world or human-interest news.
</rules>
<examples>
Article: Gaza children lose in games against real bullets — Karam Abu Najib (14) and friends brandishing exploded bottles used in their deadly game with the Israeli military.
Topic: World

Article: Depleted Wizards stun Grizzlies — Even before it was over, Antawn Jamison and Juan Dixon were limping around the court, trying to relieve aching muscles.
Topic: Sports

Article: Casino giant in $4bn Vegas plan — Casino giant MGM Mirage is building a $4bn gambling resort complex in the centre of Las Vegas.
Topic: Business

Article: Mars rovers given six more months — NASA's Mars rovers have had their missions extended by a further six months after weathering the Martian winter solstice.
Topic: Tech
</examples>
<output_format>
Respond with exactly one word — the category, capitalized exactly as one of World, Sports, Business, or Tech — and nothing else. No punctuation, no explanation.
</output_format>
